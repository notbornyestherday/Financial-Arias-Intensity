# Reference tables

Small, hand-maintained, committed. Not vendor data.

- `fomc_dates.csv`: FOMC announcement days with release time. Needed for H1b (FOMC days
  excluded) and H1c (FOMC days as a positive control). Fill in during Week 1-2 from the
  Federal Reserve's website; include unscheduled announcements made during market hours.

Event days and market-wide halt days live in `src/fai/config.py`.
